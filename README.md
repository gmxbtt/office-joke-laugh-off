One original one-line joke about office life, entered in make.fun's "One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off" ($3 in USDC). A judge model reads the jokes two at a time and keeps the funnier one — so this entry is built around one sharp, instantly-landing punchline: the corporate euphemism "growth opportunity" as a substitute for a raise.

## The joke

> I asked for a raise and got a 'growth opportunity' instead — turns out growth means doing two jobs for one salary.

## Why this joke

The field is crowded with office-plant jokes, "we're a family here" lines and "per my last email" gags. This one takes a different, specific piece of office language — the "growth opportunity" handed out instead of a raise — and twists it in the second half: growth turns out to mean more work for the same pay. Setup and punchline in a single breath, no explaining needed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the joke (114 characters, one line) to `/output/entry.txt`.
